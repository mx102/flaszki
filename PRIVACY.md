# Flaszki privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Flaszki collects **no data**.

- No analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no ads, no accounts, no tracking.
- Your progress is saved only on your device (in browser storage on the web).
  It is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data on the
  web, deletes it.
- The app needs no network access, except that the web version is loaded from
  Cloudflare Pages, which may keep standard server logs under its own policy.
- App stores (e.g. Google Play) may show the developer anonymous crash and
  usage statistics under their own policies; Flaszki adds nothing to them.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/mx102/flaszki/issues.
